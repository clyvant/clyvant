# Compliance notes — psychology content and (if ever used) supplement promotion

_Research summary, 2 Nov 2026. Not legal advice; the product sections need a lawyer's sign-off before
any product video is scripted. Sources were collected via web search; where a figure could not be
confirmed it is marked **unverified**._

## A. Psychology / human-nature content (both markets)

- **No health misinformation.** TikTok keeps moderate-harm health misinformation out of the For You feed,
  and its Mental & Behavioral Health guidelines apply to psychology content. Rules we follow: no armchair
  diagnosis of named people or partners, no "signs you have X disorder", no advice to stop treatment,
  no self-harm/suicide detail. Frame everything as research findings about *people in general*.
- **Cite the study on screen** (authors, year, journal). Avoid debunked or weak claims (see PLAYBOOK §2).
  Where a finding is contested (hedonic adaptation, Ben Franklin effect, Zeigarnik, Dunning–Kruger),
  say so in the script ("small studies", "the evidence is mixed").
- **AI disclosure.** TikTok requires a label on realistic AI-generated content; our voice is synthetic, so
  turn on the AI-generated label. In the EU, the AI Act's transparency duty (Art. 50) has applied since
  2 Aug 2026 for deepfake-type content. Never clone a real person's voice or likeness.
- **Originality.** Creator Rewards excludes unoriginal, reposted, slideshow, text-only and looping
  videos. Copying a competitor's script or edit would be both a policy risk and a copyright risk.
- **Music.** Use TikTok's library sounds for personal-account posts; copyrighted tracks over one minute
  can get the video muted. The engine renders a `__nomusic.mp4` twin for adding an in-app sound.
- **Commercial content.** Anything sponsored or promoting your own product is excluded from Creator
  Rewards and must use the commercial-content toggle (US: "Promotional content" / "Paid partnership";
  France: « Publicité » or « Collaboration commerciale » in French, visible throughout).

## B. United States — dietary supplement claims (Hillside Shilajit Gummies)

**Frameworks**
- FTC *Health Products Compliance Guidance* (Dec 2022): every express or implied health claim needs
  competent and reliable scientific evidence — generally human RCTs matching the formulation and dose;
  testimonials need the same substantiation; "traditional use" is not a substitute; qualifiers like
  "may help" don't rescue an unsupported claim; disclosures must be unavoidable.
- FDA/DSHEA: structure/function claims ("supports…") require FDA notification within 30 days and the
  disclaimer *"This statement has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is
  not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."* Disease claims are prohibited.
- FTC Consumer Review Rule (Oct 2024): fake or AI-generated testimonials carry civil penalties.
- TikTok Shop US: supplements are an invite-only category needing lab reports; "sexual enhancement or
  performance" products are listed as prohibited while "testosterone boosters" appear as permitted for
  invited sellers — a contradiction that makes male-performance positioning high-risk. Diagnose/treat/
  cure/prevent claims are banned "even with disclaimers"; before/after imagery is prohibited.
  Shoppable videos must show the physical product and a face at some point (≥3 s dynamic footage);
  AI voices are banned from promotional LIVEs (2026).
- Known gaps to fix on the US listing: the title contains "Energy Stamina"; the mg figure differs
  between the site (2,400 mg) and the brief (3,150 mg).

**Avoid → safer (US)**

| Avoid | Safer |
|---|---|
| boosts testosterone, natural TRT, "nature's Viagra", alpha | drop entirely |
| libido, stamina, performance, last longer, sperm/fertility | "part of my morning routine" |
| cures/treats/prevents fatigue, ED, anxiety, inflammation, brain fog | label-matching ingredient facts: "shilajit extract with ashwagandha, sea moss, lion's mane" |
| detox, immunity boost, fat burn, GLP-1-like | "0 g added sugar · Halal · gluten-free" |
| clinically proven, doctor/FDA approved | "third-party lab-tested, COA shown on screen" |
| 100% pure/safe, no side effects, guaranteed, results in 7 days | "tested for lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury" (only if on the COA) |
| "it dissolves, so it's real" | "home tests can't prove purity; read the lab report" |
| before/after bodies, "Day 1 vs Day 30" | taste, texture and routine shots |

Any structure/function line ("supports energy") needs dose-matched evidence plus the FDA disclaimer.

## C. France / EU — compléments alimentaires

**Frameworks**
- Reg. (EC) 1924/2006 (enforced by the DGCCRF): only *authorised* health claims may be used. Shilajit,
  ashwagandha, sea moss, burdock, lion's mane and black seed have **no authorised claim**; the formula
  contains no vitamins/minerals that carry one. "On-hold" botanical claims are no safe harbour since
  CJEU C-386/23 (30 Apr 2025). Net effect: **no health claim, stated or implied, is available** for this
  product in France. Coded wording still counts if it implies an effect.
- DGCCRF guidance: avoid verbs like *réguler, corriger, améliorer, soulager*; medical words *traitement,
  cure, symptômes, maladie*; "cliniquement/scientifiquement prouvé"; recommendations by health
  professionals; implying a balanced diet is insufficient. Recent fines: €300k and €100k for supplements
  marketed with "medical virtues".
- **ANSES opinion on ashwagandha (2021-SA-0077, 2024):** not recommended for people with thyroid, liver
  or heart disease or hyperandrogenism, pregnant women, anyone on sedative/CNS-depressant treatment, and
  (precaution) breastfeeding women and children. Not a ban; ANSES set no safe dose. Recommended caption
  line: « Contient de l'ashwagandha : déconseillé aux femmes enceintes ou allaitantes, aux moins de 18 ans,
  et en cas de troubles thyroïdiens, hépatiques, cardiaques, d'hyperandrogénie ou de traitement sédatif. »
- ANSM/DGCCRF have warned about "aphrodisiac" foods sold via social media — male-performance framing is a
  regulator red flag.
- **Loi n° 2023-451 (influencer law)** + Ordonnance 2024-978: commercial promotion must carry
  « Publicité » or « Collaboration commerciale » (or an equivalent adapted to the format), visible for
  the whole promotion, in French; « Images retouchées » for edited faces/silhouettes; « Images virtuelles »
  for AI-generated ones; written contracts with influencers. LCEN art. 20: every online ad must be
  identifiable as such and name the advertiser.
- TikTok Shop EU: supplements are a restricted category needing approval; branded-content rules on
  supplements vary by country — check the France row before paying creators.

**Red list (never use)**: testostérone/« testo », libido, désir, aphrodisiaque, érection, impuissance,
performance(s), « au lit », virilité, puissance, endurance/vigueur (male context), fertilité, sperme,
hormones, prostate, andropause · guérir, soigner, traiter, traitement, cure, remède, prévenir, maladie,
symptôme, infection, inflammation, anxiété, dépression, insomnie, « alternative aux médicaments » ·
booster, renforcer, stimuler, améliorer, augmenter, réguler, corriger, soulager, régénérer, détox,
purifier · brûle-graisse, perte de poids, minceur, coupe-faim · miracle, garanti, 100 % efficace,
cliniquement/scientifiquement prouvé, recommandé par les médecins/pharmaciens, validé par la science,
sans danger · énergie/anti-fatigue, immunité, mémoire/concentration, anti-stress/adaptogène, sommeil,
récupération (no authorised claim exists for this formula).

**Amber (legal review first)**: « riche en minéraux / en acide fulvique » (nutrition-claim conditions),
« ancestral » paired with any effect, « je me sens plus moi-même », the EU line name
"ALPHA HOMME — Vigueur & Endurance" if used in France.

## D. Creator Rewards × commerce

Creator Rewards pays personal accounts only and excludes ads/sponsored content; TikTok Shop promotion
is commercial. Run two lanes: the psychology account for Rewards; the brand/affiliate account for product
videos with the commercial toggle on. Do not tag Shop products on Rewards videos.
